🤔 What is an Election?

Explained simply — even a 5th grader gets it!

01
🏫

Think of Your School

Just like you vote for a Class Monitor, India votes for its leaders who will take care of the whole country!

02
🗳️

Everyone Gets One Vote

Every adult Indian citizen gets exactly ONE vote. Whether you're rich or poor, your vote is equally powerful!

03
🏆

Most Votes Wins

The person who gets the most votes in an area becomes the leader (MP or MLA) of that area!

04
🔒

It's Secret & Safe

Nobody can see how you voted! The EVM machine keeps your vote completely secret and safe.
